# Third-party code and assets

## Free My TV (MIT)

Parts of Still Home 0.3 are lifted from [Free My TV](https://github.com/simbasjungles/free-my-tv) by simbasjungles: the Canvas rendering core (`Scene`, `Shaders`, `Frost`, `Spring`, `Shadows`, `Recording`, `Symbols`, `Ui`, `Spoken`, `Click`, `Backdrop`), the app catalogue and logo lifting (`HomeApps`), the Open-Meteo weather client (`HomeData`), the shelf reorder, drawer and sheet interaction code in `HomeView`, and the i18n catalogues in `app/src/main/assets/i18n`.
